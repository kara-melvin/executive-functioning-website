# About Me

**Header:**
" Borrow My Executive Functioning " 

**Description:**
"Hi, I'm Katie​.

Growing up in poverty and chaos taught me how hard it can be to manage life without guidance or support. Later, living with ADHD, CPTSD, and PMDD only added more challenges. I know what it’s like when even the “simple” things feel impossible — but I also know that with the right tools, encouragement, and a community that gets it, you can build the life you want to live.

**That’s why I created Borrow My Executive Functioning. My mission is to help people find practical strategies, supportive tools, and encouragement to create systems that actually work for them.**

Think of me as that supportive friend who shows up with a plan, a fidget toy, and a reminder that you’re not broken — and that you don’t have to figure it all out alone.

✨ If you’ve ever felt like you’re falling behind, “too much,” or completely on your own, you’re exactly who I’m here for."
