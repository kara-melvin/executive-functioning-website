Disclaimer

I am not a licensed therapist, doctor, or financial advisor. My services are designed to provide emotional support, practical guidance, advocacy, and accountability, not medical, legal, or financial advice.

Any information provided during consultations, body doubling sessions, or budgeting support is based on lived experience, research, and client collaboration. While I aim to be helpful, my work should not be used as a substitute for professional care from licensed providers.

I can assist you in finding professionals who are a good fit, and I can support you in preparing for those appointments, but I do not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or manage crisis situations.

If you are in a mental health crisis or need immediate help, please contact a licensed professional or emergency service in your area.

By using my services, you acknowledge that you understand the scope and limitations of the support I offer.

Effective date: to be announced

Your privacy matters

At Borrow My Executive Functioning LLC we are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Notice explains what data we collect, how we use it, your rights, and how we keep it secure.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following data when you interact with our services:

  • Personal information: Name, email, phone number, or any other details you provide voluntarily.
  • Service data: Notes, insights, or shared details during consultations (if applicable).
  • Usage data: Your IP address, browser type, referring pages, and other analytics info.
  • Affiliate tracking: Non-personal interaction data with third-party affiliate links.

2. How we use your information

We use the data to:

  • Deliver and improve services
  • Personalize content and recommendations
  • Communicate updates or offers
  • Track affiliate performance
  • Fulfill legal or contractual obligations

We only collect and process personal data when we have a lawful basis to do so. This may include your consent, contract performance, or legal compliance.

3. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with:

  • Trusted service providers (e.g., calendar tools, payment processors, email platforms)
  • Affiliate networks (tracking clicks, not personal identity)
  • Legal authorities when required by law

All third parties are required to safeguard your data in accordance with applicable laws.

4. Data security

We use secure, encrypted platforms where possible and restrict access to sensitive data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we recommend using caution when sharing sensitive info online.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (if processing was based on consent)
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Request data portability

To make a request, email us at borrowmyexecutivefunctioning@gmail.com. We'll respond within the time required by law (usually within 30 days).

6. International users

If you're outside the U.S., your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. We take steps to ensure your information is protected in line with international standards (including GDPR, where applicable).

7. California residents (CCPA)

If you're a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we collect and why
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of the sale of personal data (we don't sell it)

To exercise your rights, email us at borrowmyexecutivefunctioning@gmail.com.

8. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Notice or your personal data, contact:

Refund Policy

We understand that life can be unpredictable, and we want to be fair and supportive. Our goal is to make sure you're getting value from what we offer, whether it's a quick call, a digital guide, or a personalized recommendation.

Phone consultations (pay-per-minute)

Due to the immediate nature of these sessions, refunds are generally not offered once the call has been completed. However, if you experience a technical issue or feel that a significant part of the call was disrupted or unhelpful, please reach out within 3 days of the session. We'll do our best to make it right, whether that's a partial refund or a free follow-up.

Digital resources (e.g., guides, templates, toolkits)

We want these resources to genuinely help. If you find that a digital product doesn't meet your expectations or wasn't what you thought it would be, please contact us within 7 days of purchase. We may offer a partial or full refund depending on the situation. All refund decisions for digital products are made on a case-by-case basis.

Affiliate recommendations

Products purchased through affiliate links are subject to the refund policies of the third-party vendor. We are not responsible for refunds on those purchases, but we're happy to help you contact the vendor or navigate the process if needed.

Customized support or project-based services

If you book a service that involves custom work or extended support, we will outline the scope and expectations beforehand. If you're unhappy at any point, please let us know. We value communication and will work with you to resolve the issue, whether that's a revised deliverable, credit, or partial refund.

How to request a refund

Email us at borrowmyexecutivefunctioning@gmail.com with:

  • Your name
  • The service or product in question
  • Your reason for the request
  • The date of purchase or session

We aim to respond within 2 business days and process any approved refunds within 3 to 5 business days.

Kindness and fairness

We built this business to serve people who are often under-served. We trust our clients to be honest, and we promise to do the same. Abuse of the refund policy (e.g., repeated refund requests with no reasonable cause) may result in the refusal of future refunds or services.
