I can make the call, ask the questions, check availability, schedule the appointment, or get the information you need to figure out your next step. Because sometimes you don't need someone to tell you to "just do it." Sometimes you just need someone else to make the call.

FAQ

Questions you might have

A trait for many neurodivergent people (myself included) is wanting to fully understand something. So here are answers to some questions I think might come up, to help you feel clear, supported, and ready to dive in.

What exactly do you do?

I help people who feel overwhelmed (neurodivergent or not) find ways to make life more manageable. I'm part consultant, part accountability partner, and part creative resource-maker. So you don't just get advice, you get tools that actually work for your brain.

Is this therapy?

Nope! I'm not a therapist, but I am HIPAA certified! I'm more like your teammate, strategist, and cheerleader rolled into one.

If therapy is a sword, think of me as the shield that helps you hold steady day-to-day. And if therapy is the step you want to take, I can help you find someone and give you the words to bring into that first appointment.

How long does it take to get scheduled with you?

I do have a day job, so my calendar isn't wide open. But I'll always respond within 1 to 2 business days, even if it's just to let you know my upcoming availability.

How is this different from coaching?

Coaching often pushes goals in a structured way. I meet you where you are, using creativity and flexibility. It's less about hustling harder and more about making life feel doable.

Do you only work with ADHD?

Not at all! While ADHD is a big focus, I also help with executive dysfunction from things like anxiety, depression, trauma, PMDD, and burnout.

In the first five minutes or first message, if I feel like I can't help you properly, I will let you know.

How do I get started?

Easy peasy! Just reach out through the contact form, or book a first call. We'll start by talking about where you are right now and what you'd like to feel instead.

What if I don't know what's "wrong" with me?

Then you're in the right place! You can tell me your struggles in your own words, and I'll help you find the vocabulary, tools, and pathways forward.

Ready when you are

No pressure and no perfect message required. Tell me what you're trying to get done and we'll figure it out together.
