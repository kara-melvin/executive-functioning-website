Help Accessing Basic Resources

A lot of people are carrying more than usual right now, and finding help can feel overwhelming when you're already exhausted.

I'm stepping back from paid work temporarily to help people access basic resources during this period. This is not my usual offering. I do have paid services, and I'll be returning to them later this year. This is a short-term shift due to current conditions.

You don't need to explain your situation to receive support. Everyone deserves access to basic resources.

Local resources I can help find

  • Food pantries and meal support
  • Rent and housing assistance
  • Utility assistance
  • Low-cost clinics and prescription support
  • Transportation resources
  • Mental health hotlines
  • Legal aid and community services

Request help

Just your zip code and somewhere to send the results. No story required.

Your email is only used to send you what I find.

How it works

You can request help by submitting your zip code only.

No story required. No explanation needed.

I'll look up available resources in your area and send them to you.

Sometimes not having to search is the support.

Please read

This service provides help locating publicly available resources. I am not a social worker, case manager, or legal, medical, or financial professional, and this service does not guarantee eligibility or outcomes.

Information shared is based on publicly available sources and may change over time. I do my best to provide accurate and helpful information, but availability and requirements are outside of my control.

This service is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger or experiencing an emergency, please contact local emergency services or a crisis hotline.
