About Me

Borrow My Executive Functioning

Hi, I'm Katie.

Growing up in poverty and chaos taught me early how much harder life can feel when you do not have the support, guidance, or resources you need.

Later, living with ADHD gave me an even deeper understanding of what it feels like to know what you should do and still not be able to make yourself do it.

I spent years learning that sometimes the answer is not more discipline, more willpower, or trying harder. Sometimes you need a different tool, a different strategy, a little more support, or permission to do things differently.

That is why I created Borrow My Executive Functioning.

What started as a place to share practical ADHD strategies has grown into an ecosystem of tools, resources, encouragement, and community designed to make everyday life a little more workable.

Here, you can find strategies for getting things done, tiny scripts for when you cannot find the words, tools for making boring things more doable, permission to approach life differently, and plenty of encouragement along the way.

You do not need to become a different person to build a life that works for you.

You just might need some things to borrow.

  • Borrow a strategy.
  • Borrow a script.
  • Borrow a little structure.
  • Borrow a reminder.
  • Borrow some encouragement.

Whatever you need to borrow, we have it here for you.

You do not have to figure it all out alone.
