How does this work?

You're not paying me every time I dial the phone.

Sometimes the person I need isn't available, the person who answers has absolutely no idea what I'm talking about, and/or I may need to follow up.

You're paying per side quest.

One side quest = one thing you're trying to get done.

Reasonable follow-up attempts are included, because you shouldn't have to pay more just because someone didn't answer the phone.

For example, one side quest could be:

  • Schedule a dentist appointment
  • Call the health department to get specific information
  • Check if a lawyer is accepting new clients
  • Ask a business about availability
  • Find out what documents you need before an appointment
  • Call and ask the question the website somehow refuses to answer
